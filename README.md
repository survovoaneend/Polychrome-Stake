# Polychrome Stake
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="202611~2" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8a060c4d-3343-4cdb-bcef-18b1fe296278" />
Have you ever wanted to play Gold Stake but without those pesky Perishable Jokers? Have you ever wanted to play on Purple Stake ante scaling without the rest of Purple Stake getting in the way? Have you ever wanted to push yourself to the absolute limit of the challenges this game has to offer?

***Polychrome Stake*** is a mod that adds a brand new stake, with its gimmick being that its difficulty is *customizable*. It is unlocked after beating Gold Stake, and allows you to mix and match over 20 different modifiers onto your run. Each modifier is worth a set amount of points, which can be used to unlock more modifiers or just compete against your friends!

For reference, Gold Stake rules is worth 700 points. The maximum that Polychrome Stake can be worth with all modifiers enabled is a little under 4000.

Polychrome Stake was originally created for the mod [All in Jest](https://github.com/survovoaneend/All-In-Jest), but was also released as a standalone mod for those that did not want to play the new difficulty with the mountain of other stuff All in Jest adds.

Enjoy!
